GETTING TO IBADAN
Lagos to Ibadan by train
The train runs from Mobolaji Johnson Station in Ebute Metta to Moniya Station in Ibadan in two and a half to three hours. On Fridays and Saturdays it leaves Lagos at 7:15 AM, 1:40 PM and 4:00 PM, with Regular class from ₦3,600. Allow time for the drive from Moniya to the ICC, and confirm times with the Nigerian Railway Corporation before you travel.
