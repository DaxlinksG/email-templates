We are proud to announce Dr. Vincent O. Olatunji, National Commissioner and CEO of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), as a speaker at ISF Forum 2026.
He is a technology policy expert with over three decades across ICT, policy and digital transformation. Before the NDPC, he spent more than a decade at NITDA, where he helped shape the Cloud First Policy and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation.
At the NDPC he champions responsible data governance, privacy and digital trust as foundations for Nigeria's digital economy.
If you build products, hold customer data or are growing a business, this is a voice worth hearing in person.
