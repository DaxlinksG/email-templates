WHO YOU WILL MEET
What they have built, and what to ask them
HOST STATE
His Excellency Seyi Makinde
Executive Governor, Oyo State
Governor of Oyo State, home of ISF Forum, which has grown here from 500 people in 2022 to 3,000 in 2024.
Ask him: what does Oyo State need from founders and investors next?
TWO UNICORNS
Iyin Aboyeji
Founding Partner, Future Africa
Co-founded Andela and Flutterwave, two of Africa's billion-dollar companies, before starting Future Africa to back the next wave. Officer of the Order of the Niger.
Ask him: what did Andela and Flutterwave get right early?
35 MILLION USERS
Chika Nwosu
Managing Director, PalmPay
Leads PalmPay in Nigeria, which serves more than 35 million registered users, and led its nationwide “PalmPay Dey for You” push on safer payments.
Ask him: how do you build trust at 35 million users?
NATIONAL TECH POLICY
Kashifu Inuwa Abdullahi
Director-General, NITDA
Heads the agency driving Nigeria's plan to train three million people in tech skills, including AI, and told the GITEX stage in Dubai that Nigeria's real strength is its people.
Ask him: where are the opportunities for founders in Nigeria's digital economy?
$43M FUND
Satoshi Shinada
Partner and Co-founder, Verod-Kepple Africa Ventures
One of the partners behind a $43 million pan-African fund backing Series A and B startups, with Moove Africa and Koko Networks in its portfolio.
Ask him: what makes a startup ready for Series A?
EARTHSHOT FINALIST
Olugbenga Olubanjo
Founder and CEO, Reeddi
One of 15 finalists in the first year of Prince William's Earthshot Prize, for Reeddi's solar battery rentals that bring power to homes and businesses.
Ask him: how do you build hardware that scales in Africa?
ASHOKA FELLOW
Dr. Abiodun Adereni
Founder and CEO, HelpMum Africa
Founded HelpMum to reduce maternal and infant deaths, reaching more than 200 rural communities with tools like an AI vaccination tracker. The first Fellow backed by Ashoka and Lenovo.
Ask him: how do you scale impact and still raise funding?
FINTECH BUILDER
Babatunde Akin-Moses
Chief Executive Officer, Sycamore
Co-founded Sycamore, which grew from digital lending into a wider financial-services platform, and is named on the Borderless 100 list.
Ask him: how do you grow a lender into a platform?
