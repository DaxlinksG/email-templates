KEYNOTE SPEAKER ANNOUNCED
Hon. Bimbo Adekanmbi, FCCA, will deliver the keynote
We are pleased to announce Hon. Abimbola (Bimbo) Adekanmbi as keynote speaker at ISF Forum 2026. A chartered accountant and public administrator, he previously served as Oyo State's Deputy Chief of Staff and Commissioner for Finance, Budget and Planning, with a career spanning international consulting and senior public service.
He is a passionate advocate for economic diversification, youth empowerment and business-friendly environments that prepare local talent to compete globally. If you are building a business in Nigeria, this is a keynote to hear in person.
