POV, in collaboration with ISF Forum, is convening a private roundtable for growth-stage founders and ecosystem leaders in Ibadan and the wider South-West.
It is part of OpenLane, a POV initiative supported by the UK–Nigeria Tech Hub, exploring how Nigerian companies access international investment, customers and commercial partnerships, with a focus on the UK.
The conversation is about you: your international ambitions, your interest in the UK or the markets you are prioritising instead, and the opportunities and barriers you have met so far.
WHO SHOULD EXPRESS INTEREST
Founders of tech-enabled companies in Ibadan with established commercial traction, preferably at least US$10,000 in monthly recurring revenue.
Investors and ecosystem leaders actively supporting growth-stage companies.
Priority goes to organisations with active operations in Ibadan.
HOW IT WORKS
Participation is strictly by invitation. We review every expression of interest, and selected applicants are contacted directly with confirmation and venue details.
